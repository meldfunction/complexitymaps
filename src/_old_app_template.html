About the live library. Resources are read on each visit from the public resource feed of the Systems Change Learning Guide by Jaya Ramchandani and Raisa Mirza, shown unmodified with its own learning-area tags (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0). This page only reads that feed. It never sends queries, conversations, or personal details anywhere. A copy is kept in your browser for an hour to spare their server.

Matching. Resources are suggested for a pathway by the people, practices, and terms named in it, plus a crosswalk to the Guide's learning areas. Matches are a starting point, not an endorsement.
